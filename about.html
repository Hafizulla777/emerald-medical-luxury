About Emerald Medical Luxury

A legacy of clinical excellence, compassionate care, and unwavering commitment to the communities we serve.

Our Story

Trusted Healthcare for Generations

Emerald Medical Luxury is a reputed multispecialty hospital dedicated to delivering advanced medical treatment with genuine compassion. Founded on the belief that every patient deserves world-class care, we combine cutting-edge technology with a patient-first philosophy across every specialty.

From emergency services to complex surgeries, preventive health screenings to chronic disease management, our team of board-certified specialists works collaboratively to deliver personalized treatment plans tailored to each patient's needs.

Heritage

Our History

Milestones that define our journey of excellence.

1998

Foundation Laid

Emerald Medical Luxury established as a small clinic with a vision to provide affordable, quality healthcare to the local community.

2005

Full-Service Hospital

Expanded into a multispecialty hospital with departments in cardiology, neurology, orthopedics, and general medicine.

2012

Advanced Diagnostics

Inaugurated a fully automated diagnostic laboratory and radiology center equipped with MRI and CT imaging technology.

2020

Critical Care Expansion

Opened a 24-bed intensive care unit and modernized operation theatres for complex surgical procedures.
